What you can paste

  • Headings: # title / section, ## ruled GPAI section, ### sub-section.
  • Math: inline $F=ma$ or \(F=ma\); display $$E_k=\tfrac12 mv^2$$ or \[...\]; also \begin{aligned}…\end{aligned}, cases, pmatrix etc. (KaTeX).
  • Tables: normal Markdown (GFM) tables. Narrow (2–3 col) tables fit best; wide tables auto-shrink/wrap.
  • Images: ![caption](https://…), or use the image button, or paste / drag an image into the editor — it embeds automatically and shows up as a short ![name](gnote-img:…) tag in the editor (the preview and the PDF still show the full image).
  • Links: [text](https://…) — clickable in the exported PDF.
  • Breaks: <!-- column-break --> and <!-- page-break --> on their own line force a new column/page; <!-- keep-topic --> above a section keeps that whole section in one column (the ▤ button in the editor inserts it at your cursor line).
  • Raw HTML (e.g. an inline <svg> diagram) passes through after sanitising.

Design controls

Start with a preset, then fine-tune. Design controls affect the sheet only — not this interface. You can change the sheet background colour and font (Serif is the exact GPAI original), spacing between words, after sentences and after paragraphs, and text boldness — body weight changes ordinary text, while emphasis and heading weights can be tuned separately under Typography → Emphasis.

Two flow modes live in the panel: Line flow → Whole words stops words being hyphenated across lines (“quanti-tative”), and Layout → Keep topics intact moves a section that no longer fits in a column to the top of the next one instead of starting it at the column foot. Both are off by default because the GPAI original hyphenates and splits freely; turn them on when you prefer the cleaner look. The GPAI preset (and a full reset) restores the original look exactly: white sheet, serif, single sentence spacing.

Notes, autosave & undo

  • Everything — content, note names, design — is saved automatically in this browser. No account, and refreshing or closing the tab never loses work.
  • Notes (top bar) creates, opens, renames and deletes notes. The name above the editor renames the open note, and it also becomes the exported PDF / .md filename.
  • Undo / redo buttons (or Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z / Ctrl+Y) step through up to 3 edits in each direction — and they survive a reload.
  • The moon button toggles dark mode for the interface. The sheet and the exported PDF always keep exactly the colours you designed.

Getting a perfect PDF

Click Export (above the preview), then in the print dialog choose Save as PDF, paper A4, Margins: None, Scale: 100%, enable Background graphics, disable headers/footers. The PDF keeps selectable text, vector math and clickable links.
